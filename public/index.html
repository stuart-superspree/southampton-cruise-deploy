      Help & safe spots

      You're never far from someone friendly.

      Southampton's a welcoming city. If you need a hand, here's how to find it.

      If it's urgent

      Call for help

      999 Emergency. Police, fire, ambulance. 101 Hampshire Police, non emergency.

      Ask for Angela

      In any pub or bar, ask for "Angela"

      Southampton pubs and bars take part in the Ask for Angela scheme. If you feel uncomfortable, unsafe, or just want to leave quietly, walk up to any member of bar staff and ask for "Angela". They'll discreetly help you slip away, call a taxi, or stay with you until you're sorted. No questions, no fuss.

      Friendly faces

      Safe spots near you

      Staffed venues where someone can help, lend a phone, or just give you a glass of water.

        Walking back later

        After dark, stick to the busy streets

        Above Bar Street and the High Street stay lit and busy until late. After sunset, we'd suggest heading back through WestQuay's main concourse rather than along Western Esplanade.

        Lost or stranded

        Getting back to your ship

        Licensed black taxis queue at the WestQuay south entrance and beside the Civic Centre. Your cruise line keeps a port agent on duty all day at the terminal. Their number is in your daily programme, worth a photo on your phone before you set off.

        Travelling with company

        Share your plan

        Send the link to this app to anyone in your party. You'll all see the same tour, the same stops, and the same return route, so it's easy to meet at the next Loop if you get separated.
